Research

Research

I estimate greenhouse gas emissions from the top down, combining atmospheric observations with transport and inverse models to see what is really being emitted and to test national inventories and climate policy.

Current project

ENCORE

Project page →

At the University of Galway I work on ENCORE, a project hosted at the Mace Head atmospheric research station on the west coast of Ireland. My focus is nitrous oxide (N2O), a long-lived greenhouse gas and the leading ozone-depleting emission today.

I use atmospheric N2O observations together with transport and inverse modelling to estimate emissions and how they change over time.

  • N2O
  • Mace Head
  • Inverse modelling
Horizon Europe

PARIS: Black carbon (WP7)

In the Horizon Europe project PARIS, I work in Work Package 7 on black carbon, running inversions that estimate black carbon emissions over Europe from atmospheric observations.

I led two of the work package's deliverables, D7.2 and D7.4.

  • Black carbon
  • European inversions
  • Led D7.2 & D7.4
