Research

Remote sensing, fire science, and atmospheric modeling

My research combines satellite remote sensing, computational modeling, and machine learning to understand wildfires, air pollution, and land use change at global scales.

🔥 Satellite-Based Wildfire Monitoring

Developing near-real-time algorithms for active fire detection and burned area mapping using MODIS, VIIRS, and Sentinel-2 multi-sensor fusion with deep learning.

Our methods improve detection in cloud-covered and tropical regions where traditional thresholding approaches underperform.

Liu et al. (2024) Code Dataset

🌫️ Smoke Plume Transport & Air Quality

Using satellite AOD, HYSPLIT trajectories, and chemical transport models to track wildfire smoke and estimate surface-level PM2.5 concentrations with XGBoost and neural networks.

We combine GOES-16 geostationary observations with TROPOMI column measurements for hourly smoke tracking across North America.

Liu et al. (2023) Web App

🌿 Land Use & Land Cover Change

Mapping deforestation and agricultural expansion using Landsat/Sentinel time-series classification with temporal CNNs and random forests on Google Earth Engine.

Paper (2023) Code

🛠️ Open-Source Geospatial Tools

Building accessible applications on Google Earth Engine and Python for environmental monitoring. I believe open science accelerates discovery and enables equitable access to data.
