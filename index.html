👋 About Me

I am a climate scientist and researcher at the University of Galway, working at the intersection of atmospheric modelling and observations, with a focus on inverse modelling methods.

My current research focuses on estimating greenhouse gas emissions using a top-down (inverse modelling) approach. I study emissions of potent greenhouse gases both long-lived species (e.g., CFCs, HFCs, N2O) and short-lived species (e.g., black carbon, propane).

I received my Ph.D. in Atmospheric Science from Scuola Superiore Studi Pavia (IUSS), Italy in 2025, and my integrated B.S.–M.S. in Physics from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Mohali, India in 2021.

🌫️ Smoke Plume Visualization

Interactive Gaussian Plume Model

This simplified Gaussian plume dispersion model illustrates how wind speed affects the transport and dilution of wildfire smoke. The visualization simulates PM2.5 concentration downwind from a fire source.

  • Model: Gaussian dispersion (Pasquill-Gifford)
  • Source: Point source wildfire emission
  • Variable: Wind speed (m/s)
  • Output: Relative PM2.5 concentration
